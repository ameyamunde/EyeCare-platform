# EyeCare – Intelligent Eye Care Technology Platform

**An integrated digital ecosystem empowering eye doctors, ophthalmologists, eye hospitals and clinics through intelligent technology, connected applications and AI-enabled healthcare solutions.**

---

## 1. About EyeCare

EyeCare is an AI-enabled eye-care technology platform conceptualised to transform how eye-care professionals manage clinical workflows, interact with patients and leverage technology in delivering eye-care services.

Designed primarily for **ophthalmologists, optometrists, eye hospitals and eye clinics**, EyeCare aims to bring essential digital capabilities together within a unified ecosystem.

The platform envisions simplifying day-to-day clinical operations, improving patient engagement, supporting efficient practice management and enabling data-driven decision-making through intelligent digital tools.

With an integrated web and Android application architecture, EyeCare is being developed with scalability, accessibility, interoperability and future AI integration in mind.

**Our vision is to make intelligent eye-care technology more accessible, connected and efficient for healthcare professionals and the institutions they serve.**

## 3. Product Vision & Objectives

EyeCare aims to build a connected eye-care technology ecosystem around the following objectives:

- **Digitise Eye-care Workflows:** Streamline routine clinical and administrative processes.
- **Improve Patient Engagement:** Enable better digital interactions between eye-care professionals and patients.
- **Enable Intelligent Healthcare:** Integrate AI capabilities to support selected healthcare workflows.

- **Support Scalable Healthcare Delivery:** Build a technology foundation suitable for individual practitioners, clinics and larger healthcare institutions.
- **Enable Data-driven Insights:** Support meaningful operational and practice-level insights.

## 4. Platform Architecture

EyeCare follows a connected application architecture with a shared backend and database layer.

### Application Ecosystem

**1. Web Application**
- Designed for eye doctors, clinics and hospital users.
- Provides access to platform functionality through a browser.
- Intended to support professional and administrative workflows.

**2. Android Mobile Application**
- Extends platform accessibility to Android devices.
- Designed to support convenient access to relevant application features.
- Shares the backend infrastructure with the web application.

**3. Shared Backend & Database**
- Centralised backend architecture.
- Shared data layer for web and mobile applications.
- Designed for scalability, security and future integrations.

**4. Cloud Infrastructure**
- AWS is the selected managed cloud provider.
- AWS Mumbai is the proposed initial deployment region, subject to applicable data-residency and regulatory requirements.

## 6. AI Integration Strategy

Artificial Intelligence is an integral part of the EyeCare product vision.

The platform is being designed with an integrated AI strategy to explore opportunities such as:

- Intelligent assistance for healthcare professionals.
- Workflow automation and productivity enhancement.
- AI-powered analysis and insights, where clinically validated and appropriate.
- Improved access to relevant information.
- Intelligent patient engagement and communication.

AI-enabled functionality will be developed with appropriate clinical oversight, validation, privacy safeguards and human supervision.

AI outputs are not intended to replace professional medical judgement.
